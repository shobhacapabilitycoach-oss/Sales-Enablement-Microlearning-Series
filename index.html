Sales Enablement Microlearning Series

A 5-module microlearning experience developed in Articulate Rise 360 to help sales professionals strengthen customer conversations, questioning skills, solution selling, objection handling, and closing techniques.

Modules

  • Module 1 – The First 60 Seconds Matter
  • Module 2 – Ask Better Questions, Sell Better Solutions
  • Module 3 – Position Value, Not Features
  • Module 4 – Handling Objections with Confidence
  • Module 5 – Closing with Clarity and Commitment

Tools Used
