Sales Enablement Microlearning Series

Project Overview

Sales Enablement Microlearning Series is a five-module learning experience designed to help sales professionals improve customer conversations, uncover needs through effective questioning, position value-based solutions, handle objections, and close with confidence.

Developed in Articulate Rise 360, the series applies microlearning principles and learner-centered design to deliver focused, engaging, and practical learning experiences.

Modules

  • Module 1 – The First 60 Seconds Matter
  • Module 2 – Ask Better Questions, Sell Better Solutions
  • Module 3 – Position Value, Not Features
  • Module 4 – Handling Objections with Confidence
  • Module 5 – Closing with Clarity and Commitment
